# Connect Iqbal Saku

Open Supabase → SQL Editor → New query. Paste the complete contents of schema.sql and click Run.

Then open Authentication → URL Configuration:
- Site URL: https://iqbalsaku.pages.dev/
- Redirect URLs: https://iqbalsaku.pages.dev/

Keep email/password enabled. After the app update is deployed, open Settings → Online sync → Create account, confirm your email, then sign in. Import your expense JSON backup after signing in. Use the same account on your phone and computer.

Do not put database passwords or secret/service_role keys in the app.
